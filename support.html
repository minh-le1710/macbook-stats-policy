Frequently Asked Questions

How do I move and reposition the HUD overlay? ▼

By default, the HUD overlay is locked so that your mouse clicks pass completely through to whatever game, code editor, or app is running underneath.

  1. Press ⌘L or click the Menu Bar icon and select "Unlock Position (⌘L)".
  2. A subtle dashed border and drag handle will appear around the HUD.
  3. Click and drag the overlay to your preferred position anywhere on your screen (or secondary monitors).
  4. Press ⌘L again to lock it in place.
How do I change themes and HUD styles? ▼

Open Settings (⌘,) by clicking the speedometer icon in your macOS Menu Bar:

  • Display Engines: Choose between Classic RTSS (compact retro gamer style), Liquid Glass (frosted modern macOS pill), or Sparkline Graph (rolling 60s activity graphs).
  • Theme Presets: Choose from 14 curated palettes including Cyberpunk 2077, Tokyo Night, Monokai Pro, OLED Pure Black, Dracula, and Apple Silicon Minimal.
  • Menu Bar Customization: Choose from 5 distinct Menu Bar representations: Custom Gauge, Dynamic Speedometer, Gauge + CPU %, Gauge + CPU & RAM, or Dual Activity Bars.
Which Mac models and macOS versions are supported? ▼

MacBook Stats Overlay is built as a Universal binary for:

  • Apple Silicon: Full native support for M1, M1 Pro/Max/Ultra, M2 series, M3 series, and M4 series chips (including distinct P-Core vs E-Core telemetry and unified GPU memory allocation).
  • Intel Macs: Supports Intel Core i5/i7/i9 Macs running macOS 13.0 Ventura or higher.
  • macOS Compatibility: macOS 13.0 (Ventura), macOS 14.0 (Sonoma), and macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or newer.
Does the app cause battery drain or high CPU usage? ▼

No. MacBook Stats Overlay is written in pure native Swift and Metal with zero Electron or webview overhead.

  • Typical CPU usage is under 0.8% on a single efficiency core.
  • Memory footprint is lightweight (~18 MB to 35 MB RAM).
  • Zero GPU rendering load when unchanged, drawing at 60 FPS only during active HUD animations.
  • Battery telemetry reads real-time instantaneous discharge wattage (InstantAmperage × Voltage) directly from the AppleSmartBattery controller so you can monitor your Mac's battery health accurately.
How does the Lifetime Pro unlock work? ▼

MacBook Stats Overlay is free to use forever for essential hardware monitoring. If you'd like to support development and unlock advanced features:

  • You can purchase the Lifetime Pro Unlock ($2.99 USD) directly inside the app.
  • It is a one-time non-consumable purchase (NOT a subscription).
  • You can restore your purchase anytime on any new or secondary Mac signed into your Apple ID by clicking "Restore Purchases" in Settings.
Why does the overlay show on top of full-screen games? ▼

The HUD overlay window is configured at the macOS floating screen saver / status level (NSWindow.Level.floating) with click-through enabled. For games running in borderless windowed or full-screen windowed mode, the overlay will remain cleanly visible above game visuals.
